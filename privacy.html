Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Niyyah ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. We built Niyyah with privacy as a priority — the App does not require you to create an account, and we collect only what is necessary to provide core features.

Information We Collect

Location Data. Niyyah requests access to your device's location in order to calculate accurate prayer times for your area. Prayer times are calculated entirely on your device. To show your city name and choose a calculation method suited to your country, your coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service, which returns a place name. Your location is stored only on your device — we do not operate servers and never receive it. Location access can be disabled at any time in your device settings, though this will prevent automatic prayer time calculation.

No Account Required. Niyyah does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide an email address, name, or any personal identifiers to use the App. Your usage (bookmarks, preferences, progress) is stored locally on your device unless otherwise noted below.

Subscription & Purchase Information. If you purchase a subscription or in-app product, this transaction is processed by Apple's App Store and by RevenueCat, our subscription management provider. RevenueCat may collect a device identifier and purchase/transaction data to manage your subscription status and entitlements. We do not receive or store your payment details (such as card numbers) — these are handled entirely by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy for details on their data practices.

How We Use Information

  • To calculate and display accurate prayer times based on your location
  • To manage and validate active subscriptions and unlock premium features

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Limited data leaves your device only in the cases described above: your coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to look up your city, and subscription data is handled by Apple and RevenueCat. In each case this is solely to the extent necessary to provide the service. Niyyah contains no advertising or analytics.

Data Retention

Since Niyyah does not require an account, most of your data (saved duas, preferences, streaks, settings, and your location for prayer times) is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App. Subscription records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat per their respective retention policies.

Children's Privacy

Niyyah is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below so we can address it.

Your Choices

  • You can disable location access at any time via your device's Settings app.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored data.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription via your Apple ID account settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
